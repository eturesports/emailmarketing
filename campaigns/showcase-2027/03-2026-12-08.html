Quick answers

Do I book the hotel?
No. Eture books your Showcase nights. Extra nights before or after go directly with the hotel.

Can I share a room with a coach from another program?
Yes. A shared room is two coaches, from your staff or from another university. Tell us when you register.

Is airfare included?
No. Fly into Valencia (VLC), or into Madrid and take the high-speed train to Valencia. We pick you up at the airport or the train station.
