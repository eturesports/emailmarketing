The players

Every player on the field comes through our recruiting agency or our residential development program in Spain, so our staff knows each player's game, character and goals.

✓  Spanish players developed in academy football and in senior competition with Eture FC. Today, 40% of the Spanish players in NCAA Division I came through Eture.

✓  American players from our gap-year residential program, who return to the NCAA after a full season of senior football in Spain. Recent examples include Aidan Ahmad (Cal), Isaac Rosenthal (Northwestern), Nicholas Brown (Dartmouth) and Zolly Corrigan (Louisville).

✓  International players who complete the same program in Spain.
