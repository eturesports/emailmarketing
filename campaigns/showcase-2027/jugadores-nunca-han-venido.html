We are writing with an update on the players who will take part in the Eture Showcase 2027, to be held in Valencia, Spain, from 8 to 11 February. More than 150 players will compete over two days of games.

Every player on the field comes through our recruiting agency or our residential development program in Spain, so our staff knows each player's game, character and goals. In 2026, 68 college programs attended, from NCAA Division I to the NJCAA.

Spanish talent

Players developed in Spanish academy football and in senior competition with Eture FC, with daily training at our Valencia and Vinaròs campuses. Today, 40% of the Spanish players in NCAA Division I came through Eture.

American talent

Players from our gap-year residential program, who spend a full season in Spain training daily and competing in senior football before returning to the NCAA. Recent examples include Aidan Ahmad (Cal), Isaac Rosenthal (Northwestern), Nicholas Brown (Dartmouth) and Zolly Corrigan (Louisville).

International talent

Players from other countries who complete the same program in Spain, with the academic preparation to arrive eligible.
